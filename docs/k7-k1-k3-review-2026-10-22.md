## 6. Timing-sensitive test diagnosis and flakiness assessment — PASS WITH FINDINGS

### Assessment of K3 Record Diagnosis

The timing failure in `tests/copilot/test_k7_wave2x.py::test_an_answer_written_at_the_bound_is_reviewed_and_settled`
occurred during K3's full test suite runs, where pytest reported `assert [] == ['absence_inference']`.
Trace logs (`/tmp/k3-current-prefix-trace.log`) recorded:
`BOUND_TRACE {'steps': 2, 'elapsed': 0.95098s, 'final_prefix': 'Next I will look at l2. ', 'exhausted': ['wall_time'], 'answered': False}`.

**Analysis**:
The test configures `kiwi_max_wall_seconds=0.5`. It supplies three turns:
1. `investigating("f1", failures())` (tool call + execution)
2. `investigating("l2")` (tool call + execution)
3. `ScriptedTurn(text=ABSENT, delay=5.0)` (diagnosis turn designed to timeout during streaming)

Under heavy test suite concurrency or scheduling delay, executing Turns 1 and 2 exceeded
0.500s (taking 0.951s). At line 1312 of `service.py`, `state.out_of_turns()` detected
`elapsed >= max_wall_seconds` at the conclusion of Turn 2, terminating the loop and
calling `_bounded_fallback(..., answered=False)` before Turn 3 was ever reached. Consequently,
the diagnosis `ABSENT` was never processed, and findings were empty `[]`.

The K3 record correctly noted the 0.95s elapsed time at step 2, but hypothesized that K1's
interposition of an async generator in the disabled fallback path contributed to the failure.
However, because Turn 3 was never reached, that path was never executed; the root cause is
the excessively narrow 500ms wall-time budget across two asynchronous ASGI round-trips and
mock lookup executions.

### Flakiness Assessment and Recommended Fix

- **Flakiness**: The test remains flaky on loaded systems or slower hardware whenever
  Turns 1 and 2 take longer than 500ms combined.
- **Recommended Fix (DO NOT APPLY in this task)**:
  In `tests/copilot/test_k7_wave2x.py`, increase `kiwi_max_wall_seconds` from `0.5` to `2.0`
  (or `3.0`), keeping Turn 3's delay at `5.0s`. Alternatively, use a simulated/mock clock
  fixture to decouple wall-time expiry from real elapsed execution time.
